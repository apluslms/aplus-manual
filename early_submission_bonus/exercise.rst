Test the early bonus
====================

This questionnaire is a quick way to test the module's early submission bonus.
Because the bonus is configured on this module, it applies to this questionnaire
and any other assignment added here.

Submit before **2030-12-31 12:00** to earn 10% extra points. Submissions at or
after that time, but before the module closes, are accepted without the bonus.
